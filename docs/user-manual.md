# AWM Chat — User Manual

*For all Ascot Wealth Management staff · Last updated 21 May 2026*

---

## Read this first — AWM Chat is a living tool

AWM Chat launches **deliberately lean**. It does a few things well today, and
**more capability is added as the team needs it** — not all at once.

Features such as **document and image upload**, and **integration with internal
company apps**, are examples of things that will be built in **as required**.

So if AWM Chat doesn't yet do something you need, that's expected — it isn't a
fault or an oversight. It means we haven't been asked for it yet. Tell us, and
it goes on the list (see **Section 10 — Requesting features**).

---

## 1. What AWM Chat is

AWM Chat is Ascot Wealth Management's own private assistant, powered by Claude.
It is a secure place to ask work questions and get help — **without sending
company information to public AI tools**. Every conversation stays inside AWM's
own systems.

Typical uses:

- Drafting and refining emails, notes, and documents
- Summarising or explaining long or technical material
- Thinking through a problem or checking your reasoning
- Quick answers to work questions

It is a **work tool only**.

## 2. Signing in

1. Go to **chat.chimerasportstrading.com**
2. Click **Continue with Google**
3. Sign in with your **@ascotwm.com** Workspace account — no other accounts can access the platform

The first time you sign in you'll be asked to read and accept a short set of
ground rules (below). You only do this once.

To **sign out**, use the exit icon at the top-right of the screen — do this when
you've finished on a shared or public device.

## 3. Ground rules (please read)

AWM Chat is a company system. By using it you accept that:

- **Conversations are company property.** They belong to Ascot Wealth Management and may be reviewed for compliance, training, and operational purposes.
- **It is for work only.** Don't use it for personal matters or private correspondence.
- **No client identifiers.** Do not paste live client personal data — full names tied to financial details, account numbers, ID numbers — unless that specific workflow has been explicitly authorised.
- **There is an audit trail.** Your conversations are confidential to you day-to-day, but compliance can review them, and any such access is logged.

To help everyone stay within the rules, AWM Chat automatically flags messages
containing certain sensitive terms (for example "account number" or "ID number")
for compliance review — another good reason to keep client identifiers out.

## 4. Having a conversation

The main screen is your chat window.

- Type your question or request in the box at the bottom (**"Message Claude…"**).
- Press **Enter** to send. Press **Shift + Enter** to add a new line within your message.
- The reply appears as it is written. Claude replies in **formatted text** — headings, lists, tables and code all display properly.
- Click **New conversation** (top-left) to start a fresh, separate thread.

A reminder sits under the message box: **Claude can make mistakes.** Always
verify anything critical independently — figures, regulatory points, and
client-specific detail in particular.

## 5. Managing your conversations

Your conversations are listed down the left-hand side:

- They are grouped automatically by recency — **Today, This week, This month, Older**.
- Use the **Search** box to find a past conversation by its title.
- Click any conversation to reopen it — your history is saved.
- To remove one, hover over it, click the **⋯** menu, and choose **Delete**.

## 6. Pinned context

Open the **Pinned context** panel using the button at the top-right. Anything
you pin here is **automatically included in every conversation** with Claude, so
you never have to repeat it.

Good things to pin:

- Your role and team
- Projects you are currently working on
- How you like answers formatted (brief or detailed, British English, and so on)
- Recurring background Claude should always keep in mind

To add one: click **Add pinned item**, give it an optional label, write the
context, and click **Pin**. Remove a pin with the **×** when it's no longer needed.

## 7. Your monthly allowance

Each person has a monthly usage allowance, measured in "tokens" (broadly, the
amount of text exchanged). Your current usage is shown in the top bar.

- Normal day-to-day use stays comfortably within the allowance.
- If you reach the limit, the message box is disabled until the next calendar month — or contact your administrator to raise your cap.

## 8. Tips for better results

- **Give context.** The more Claude knows about the task, the better the answer. Use Pinned context for things that rarely change.
- **Be specific.** "Draft a client email explaining X in plain English, around 150 words" beats "write an email".
- **Iterate.** If a reply isn't quite right, say what to change — Claude remembers the conversation.
- **One topic per conversation.** Start a new conversation when you change subject; it keeps each thread focused.
- **Always verify.** Check facts, figures, and any compliance or regulatory detail for yourself.

## 9. For administrators

Administrators see a **gear icon** in the top bar, which opens the admin area:

- **Users** — everyone's usage; set each person's monthly **token cap** and the **AI model** they use.
- **Metrics** — active users, total conversations, tokens used, and estimated spend this month.
- **Flagged content** — messages caught by the sensitive-keyword check, for compliance review.
- **Settings** — the default AI model for new users, the list of selectable models, the default token cap, and the keyword-flag list.

document or image, pulling in data from another internal system, a new shortcut
or workflow — **that is a feature request, not a limitation of the tool.**
